Verbs - More Essentials in Japanese — N5 words (どうし（そのほか）)

Here are 12 of the 29 verbs - more essentials words in our N5 word list, each with kanji, kana, romaji, the English meaning and a short example sentence.

JapaneseRomajiEnglish & example
あるaruto exist; to have (things)
本がたくさんあります。
Hon ga takusan arimasu.
There are many books.
いるiruto exist; to be (people, animals)
うちにいぬがいます。
Uchi ni inu ga imasu.
There is a dog at home.

いる		iruto need

ふる		furuto fall (rain, snow)
